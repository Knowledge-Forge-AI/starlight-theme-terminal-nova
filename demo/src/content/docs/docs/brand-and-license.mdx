## Two kinds of permission

Terminal Nova has a software project and a product identity. They are related, but they are not the
same permission:

| Surface | Guidance |
| :--- | :--- |
| Software and reusable theme resources | Follow the repository's `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, and any applicable commercial terms. |
| Product name, Nova Ingot marks, favicons, and custom lettering | Follow the brand-use guidance for the identity asset and its actual surface. Software distribution does not automatically grant trademark or endorsement rights. |
| Third-party material | Preserve the terms and attribution that apply to that material. |

This page documents the existing relationship. It does not change `NOTICE`, `LICENSE`, or
commercial grants, and it does not retroactively revoke permission already granted under those
terms.

## Existing approved demo artwork

The STTN demo's brand directory contains four identity SVG files: two dedicated favicons and two
Nova Ingot mark treatments. Keep their geometry, colors, and surface treatments unchanged. The
cross-site protected-SVG receipt is a separate exact set: the two Nova Ingot marks plus the
text-only `sttn-social.svg` and sibling `cttn-social.svg` social sources. The social sources are
site metadata assets, not additional Nova Ingot treatments; the favicon files remain in this
consumer's local inventory.

| File | Intended surface | Treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `favicon-on-light.svg` | Light favicon surface | Use the dedicated light favicon at small sizes |
| `favicon-on-dark.svg` | Dark favicon surface | Use the dedicated dark favicon at small sizes |
| `theme-forge-terminal-nova-mark-on-light.svg` | Light page or header surface | Standalone Nova Ingot mark for an on-light treatment |
| `theme-forge-terminal-nova-mark-on-dark.svg` | Dark page or header surface | Standalone Nova Ingot mark for an on-dark treatment |

:::caution[Do not mix mark selection with accent selection]

Generation-time accent selection concerns generated theme output. It is not permission to recolor a
Nova Ingot mark. Choose an approved asset for the surface and keep appearance-token changes around
it.

:::

<Aside type="note" title="Keep provenance discoverable">

If you copy an existing identity asset into another site or package, keep a short inventory that
names the file, intended role, source collection, and applicable guidance. Do not invent a blanket
artwork license from the fact that a generator produced an SVG.

</Aside>

## Product-site usage checklist

Before publishing a product page or package that displays the identity:

1. Pick the light/dark artwork that matches the surface.
2. Preserve the artwork bytes, proportions, and approved treatment.
3. Keep the software license and brand guidance linked separately.
4. Preserve third-party notices where they apply.
5. Avoid language that suggests endorsement or official status beyond the authorized use.

The public contact for questions about official identity use is
[sam.lair@knowledge-forge.ai](mailto:sam.lair@knowledge-forge.ai).

## Product relationship

The mark identifies the Terminal Nova family; it does not collapse the product boundaries described
in [Architecture Overview](/overview/). STTN is the Astro/Starlight target, CTTN is the sibling
shadcn/Tailwind target, and Nebular Fusion is the desktop authoring and preview workbench. Their
surrounding appearance can differ while each product keeps its own identity guidance.
